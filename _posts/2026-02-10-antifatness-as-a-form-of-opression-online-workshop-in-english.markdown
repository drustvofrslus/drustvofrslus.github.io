Spletna delavnica v angleščini // Online workshop in English

We are happy to introduce you to Jana from Czechia, an amazing soul that exudes confidence, authenticity and a positive aura.

Jana Myšáková is an educator, founder of an educational project @respektuju and a founder and creative director of a non profit community center @safespacebookstore. She hosts a podcast @thefatfrequency about fat liberation and self acceptance.

In this workshop @nottheplainjaneagain will walk you through the categories and examples of antifatness as an oppression that is systemic and not just personal. She will talk about noticing antifatness in medical care, media and more, and will tell you how you can be an ally to fat people.

We invite you to the workshop where you can learn something new about this topic, get some tips and tricks on how to become an ally and how to be vocal about fatphobia.

We will send you the link to the workshop a day prior.
